The machine holds the camera now. It frames fast, shoots tirelessly, and never balks at one more take. What it doesn't do is decide which take is the one — what the scene is *for*, what survives the cut, what the whole thing is about.

That decision was always the job. On a set, the person who never touches the camera is still answerable for every frame; software just got its version of that seat.
