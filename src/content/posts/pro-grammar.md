Ive been something of a shy writer. Not that I havent shared anything. I've published poems and short stories and wrote small screenplays for short films and published blog posts and technical writings for my own ideas as well as on the clock.
But much of my work has ended in notebooks that no one else but spirits and nosey spies have ever read.

For a long time, all I wanted to do was to be a writer. I imagained myself as the bohemeian poet, the mysterious wizard of wards, the nomadic bard, the odd horror scribe, the one that could write the movie to change the world.
I imagined myself as all these things. Going on withmy life of work and school and responsibility.
Over the years, it became more and more of a side saddle. The shape and energy of it has still never left me though.

The process and systematically piecing the story together and the research and introspection aspects of writing have seeped into many things I do.

Writing in AI, I wonder, about the authenticity of it. I don't live in the words like I do when I just write. But I am in the ideas.
I write the same way, shape the ideas and then shape the words around and through them.

Whats interesting with writing with a model is that the AI doesnt just talk about the words you use. It discusses the ideas. It lives in the imagination space as a sort of 
non deterministic metronome. 
a dimension over the idea space, like how the 5th dimension can traverse time. 

I build software and write essays with an LLM collaborator.
I kind of feel like Im the director and the AI is the producer.
The way in which I accept the output o fthe model is very different between essays and code files.
